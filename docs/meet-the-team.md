# Meet the Team

Behind every great toolbox, there's a team of highly trained ninja technicians. Ours have been forged in the fires of blue screens, green screens, and every screen in between.

## Big Tracey Ninja

**Role:** Ninja Technician, First Class

The other half of the legendary Tracey duo. Thirty-plus years of friendship, starting from the day they both simultaneously blue-screened during a Windows 95 LAN party and realised they were kindred spirits. Where Little Tracey goes, Big Tracey follows — usually carrying a longer Ethernet cable.

Big Tracey cut her teeth on desktop support back when "the internet" meant dialling up and praying. She has personally explained to more humans than she can count why "password123" is not an acceptable password, why you can't "just download more RAM," and why the CD tray is not a cup holder.

Married to IT Super Nerd — proving that love can blossom over a shared hatred of printer drivers.

**Specialist skills:** User training and patience (legendary reserves), Group Policy wrangling, Active Directory wizardry, printer troubleshooting (the dark art no one else will touch), and translating tech jargon into plain English.

**Catchphrase:** *"No, you cannot use 'password123'. Not even with a capital P."*

## Little Tracey Ninja

**Role:** Ninja Technician, First Class

Little Tracey has been best friends with Big Tracey for over 30 years — a friendship forged in the ancient fires of Windows 95 setup wizards and cemented by decades of shared trauma from "This program has performed an illegal operation."

Trained by masters in the dark art of IT — the sacred discipline of turning it off and on again — Little Tracey is a GUI warrior through and through. From the revolutionary Start button of Windows 95 to the bewildering tiles of Windows 8, from the Aero glass of Vista to the rounded corners of Windows 11, she has survived every reinvention Microsoft has thrown at the desktop.

**Specialist skills:** Control Panel navigation (all views), right-click context menu mastery, Device Manager troubleshooting, "Have you tried restarting?" counselling, desktop shortcut organisation, and detecting which toolbar the user accidentally installed from a dodgy download.

**Catchphrase:** *"Have you tried turning it off and on again?"*

## IT Nerd

**Role:** Senior Infrastructure Architect (Self-Appointed)

The father of the group. Beard: magnificent. Slippers: on. Mug: "There's no place like 127.0.0.1."

IT Nerd was configuring UNIX systems before most people had email addresses. He speaks fluent DEC VAX, IBM DB2, and AS/400 RPG. He has navigated green screen telnet sessions and 5250 emulation terminals blindfolded — and not as a party trick, but because the monitor had failed and he still had a deadline.

He remembers when MS-DOS was cutting-edge, when Windows 3.11 for Workgroups was "the future of networking," and when a 20MB hard drive was more storage than any reasonable person could ever need. He still has a working floppy drive. Just in case. You never know.

He firmly believes the command line is the only real interface and that GUIs are "just a phase the industry is going through." He has been saying this since 1995.

**Specialist skills:** UNIX/Linux system administration, IBM mainframe operations, AS/400 RPG programming, DEC VAX/VMS, MS-DOS batch scripting, Windows 3.11 networking, green screen terminal navigation, 5250 emulation, cable management (the old-school kind, with actual cable ties), and delivering withering looks when someone says "the cloud is just someone else's computer."

**Catchphrase:** *"Back in my day, we had 640K and we were grateful."*

## IT Super Nerd

**Role:** Chief Everything Officer

The brains of the operation. IT Super Nerd knows both sides of the fence — and the fence itself, its firmware version, and three ways to exploit it.

**Old school:** UNIX, mainframes, green screens, COBOL, assembly language, punch cards (he keeps one on his desk as a reminder of humility). **New school:** AWS, Kubernetes, Docker, Terraform, LLMs, generative AI, and whatever acronym was invented last Tuesday.

Networks, servers, storage, security, ERP software, business intelligence, open-source evangelism — if it has a CLI, an API, or a config file, he's already written a script for it. Probably in three languages. With unit tests.

He is the author of NinjaMenu's deep-dive educational content — the nmap walkthroughs, the network analysis tutorials, the kind of documentation that makes you feel like you've earned a certification just by reading it.

He is also married to Big Tracey, proving once and for all that nerds can find love. They bonded over a shared passion for correctly subnetting a /22 network and have been inseparable ever since.

**Specialist skills:** Everything IT Nerd knows, plus: AWS cloud architecture, Kubernetes orchestration, network security and penetration testing, storage area networks, ERP implementation, business intelligence, AI/ML pipelines, LLM prompt engineering, open-source contribution, and writing documentation that people actually want to read.

**Catchphrase:** *"I've already automated that."*
